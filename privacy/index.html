Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Misaligned Games (“we”, “us”) develops and publishes mobile and web games. This policy covers this website, our internal tools, and our games generally. Where a game collects anything beyond what is described here, that game has its own policy linked from its page.

What we collect

  • Product analytics. We use PostHog (hosted in the EU) to understand how our games are played. It collects anonymous events, an anonymous device identifier, and an approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address. We do not collect your name or precise location.
  • Install attribution. Our Android apps include the AppsFlyer SDK to measure which ad campaigns lead to installs. It collects your device advertising ID (GAID) and basic install and app-open events. Aggregate campaign results are shared with our advertising partners to measure campaigns. We do not build an advertising profile of you.
  • Email, if you give it. If you pre-register or contact us, we store the email address you submit so we can reply or notify you at launch.
  • Session replay. On capable devices we may record anonymized replays of in-app UI interactions to diagnose usability problems. These are not tied to your identity.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with anyone other than the processors named here, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Processors

PostHog (analytics, EU infrastructure), AppsFlyer (install attribution), Supabase (email storage) and Vercel (hosting) act as our data processors.

Internal tools

We operate private dashboards, used only by our own team, that read aggregate performance data from our advertising and analytics providers. These tools are not accessible to the public, hold no personal data about players, and are used solely to decide which of our games to keep developing.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to delete any data associated with you — including a pre-registration email or analytics data — by emailing hello@misaligned.games. We action requests within 30 days. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

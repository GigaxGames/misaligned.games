Privacy Policy — Purrfect Bounce

Last updated: 6 July 2026

Purrfect Bounce is developed by Misaligned Games (“we”, “us”).

Analytics

We use PostHog (hosted in the EU) to understand how the game is played and improve it. PostHog collects anonymous product-analytics events (e.g. which features you use), an anonymous device identifier, and an approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address. We do not collect your name or precise location, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Session replay

On capable devices we may record anonymized replays of your in-app UI interactions (menus, buttons, on-screen HUD) to diagnose usability issues. On higher-end devices, and never over a cellular connection, a sampled portion of sessions may also include a low-frame-rate recording of the game screen itself. These replays are not tied to your identity and contain no personal information.

Pre-registration

If you pre-register on our landing page, we also store the email address you choose to submit, so we can notify you at launch. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

Processors

PostHog (analytics and replays, EU infrastructure) and Supabase (pre-registration emails) act as our data processors. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Permissions

The app requests the INTERNET permission to send anonymous analytics events and to load its own bundled game content.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion

To opt out of analytics or request deletion of any data associated with you — including a pre-registration email you submitted — email hello@misaligned.games and we will action it. We delete the data within 30 days of your request, and retain nothing beyond that window.

Privacy Policy — Block Hero

Last updated: 6 July 2026

Block Hero is developed by Misaligned Games (“we”, “us”).

Analytics

We use PostHog (hosted in the EU) to understand how the game is played and improve it. PostHog collects anonymous product-analytics events (e.g. when a run starts and ends, and its outcome), an anonymous device identifier, and an approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address. We do not collect your name, email, or precise location, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Session replay

On a small, sampled share of sessions on higher-end devices, we may record an anonymized replay of your gameplay — the game screen and your on-screen interface interactions (menus, the backpack, the HUD) — to understand how the game plays and diagnose usability issues. These replays are not tied to your identity and contain no personal information, and we do not record anything outside the game. Most sessions and lower-end devices are never recorded.

Legal basis & processor

PostHog acts as our data processor. Event data and any session replays are stored on PostHog’s EU infrastructure.

Local storage & cloud saves

The game saves your progress and settings on your own device. If you use cloud saves, that progress is also stored with our infrastructure provider so it can sync across your devices; it is not used for advertising and is never sold.

Permissions

The app uses your device’s internet access to send anonymous analytics events and to sync cloud saves. No other personal data leaves your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

To opt out of analytics or request deletion of data associated with your device, email hello@misaligned.games and we will action it. We delete your analytics events, device identifier, approximate location, and any session replay recordings within 30 days of collection or of your request, whichever is sooner; we retain nothing beyond that window.
